 I am currently a Research Scientist at <strong>Microsoft</strong>, exploring the development of
  efficient neural network architectures for time series modeling—primarily focusing on
  forecasting, anomaly detection, and generation. I am fortunate to be advised by
  <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=9XiIwDQAAAAJ" target="_blank">Dr. Vijay Srinivas Agneeswaran</a>
  and <a href="https://badripatro.github.io/" target="_blank">Dr. Badri Narayana Patro</a> at Microsoft.

  I am nearing the completion of my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering under the supervision of
  <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/koteswarraojerripothula/home?authuser=0" target="_blank">Dr. Koteswar Rao Jerripothula</a>.
  Prior to this, I completed my Master’s and Bachelor’s degrees at the
  <a href="#">University of Delhi</a>.

  When I am not working, I usually spend time exploring ergonomic keyboards on
  <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/ErgoMechKeyboards/" target="_blank">Reddit</a> or
  convincing people why they should switch to <strong>Vim</strong>.
  I also enjoy participating in local running events and can often be found running during the early morning hours.
  Recently, I have also started working on improving my sketching using fountain pens.
